# Research: Furnished Paths

## How tools lay objects along a line

Planet Coaster lets the player turn on path extras in the path menu, so the path tool lays benches, bins, and lamps as the path is drawn. The player can also place them one at a time from the scenery menu. The slice's RESEARCH.md covers the rest of how park games handle path furniture.

Cities: Skylines 2's Line Tool places objects along a straight line, a curve, or a circle. The player sets a spacing and sees a live preview before placing. A fixed-length mode spreads the objects evenly over the whole line, at a spacing as close as it can get to the one the player set. On a circle it rounds the spacing so the objects close the loop evenly. A fence mode lays objects end to end.

What this means: a spacing the player sets, spread evenly over the length available, is the established pattern. Players expect a live preview of it. A setting on a segment works like the Line Tool's even mode, applied to each free stretch of verge.

Rejected: placing objects at exactly the set spacing from one end. It leaves an odd gap at the far end of each stretch, which looks careless next to a shop or a junction.

Sources: https://planetcoaster.com/player-guides/building-your-park — path extras laid by the path tool; https://github.com/phillycheeze/LineTool-CS2 — spacing, live preview, and fixed-length even spacing; https://nexusmods.com/citiesskylines2/mods/57 — the Line Tool's modes.

## Where street furniture may stand

Street design guides keep furniture clear of junctions and driveway openings, out of the walking width, and set back a little from the edge of the walking surface. Some ask for extra clearance within about 20 m of a junction, so people can see each other there.

What this means: furniture stands on the verge, clear of the paving guests walk on. It stops short of a junction's mouth and leaves the way into a shop or a walkway open. Those clearances are tuning values, set by playing.

Sources: https://englewoodco.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/28859/637629788776930000 — clearance at intersections and driveways; https://sevenoaks.gov.uk/info/20038/licences_and_permits/530/pavement_licence_conditions — keeping a clear walking width; https://www.york.ca/media/80681/download — furniture placement along roads.
