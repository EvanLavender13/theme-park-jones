# Research: Litter and Janitors

## How other park games handle litter, bins, and janitors

In Planet Coaster 2, guests drop litter in a bin they pass, or on the ground if they carry it too long. Bins fill, and janitors empty them. Janitors also sweep litter and vomit, and clean toilets. The player hires janitors and limits where they work with staff zones, areas drawn on the park that each have their own colour. Staff need breaks in staff rooms. Parkitect makes janitors empty bins and carry the bags to a depot or garbage chute. Its staff-only paths and utility buildings break immersion, so players hide them behind scenery. Parkitect also lets the player assign janitors to zones and tasks.

What this means: both games limit janitors by zones or tasks the player hands out. That is the kind of instruction principle 9 rules out. In this slice, where the player places a staff room, and how far its janitors walk, limits where they work. Parkitect's bags carried to a depot are the same chain as this slice's garbage, which leaves the staff room over backstage paths.

Rejected: janitor zones the player draws, as in Planet Coaster 2. They are individual instructions (principle 9). Janitors the player hires one by one. With no money in the game, nothing would limit the count.

Sources: https://planetcoaster.com/player-guides/staff — janitor jobs, staff zones, staff rooms; https://planetcoaster.com/player-guides/facilities — bins and litter; https://www.coasterforce.com/forums/threads/specialized-studios-parkitect-final-release.41239 — Parkitect bins emptied, bags hauled; https://steamcommunity.com/app/453090/discussions/1/2639605437887085413/ — Parkitect janitor zones and depots.

## How people litter

People carry litter only so far before dropping it. Disney is said to keep a bin within about 30 steps of anywhere for this reason, and a litter study found people dropped litter a mean 29 feet from the nearest bin. People also litter more where litter already lies. In Cialdini's 1990 study, 11 percent of people littered in a clean place and 41 percent in a littered one. The effect grew when they saw someone else drop litter.

What this means: a carry distance makes bin spacing the player's main lever, and it is easy to show. Litter that attracts more litter would make a neglected area get worse faster. It is a good later deepening, and the slice leaves it out.
