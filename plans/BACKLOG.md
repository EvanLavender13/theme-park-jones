- Preview without copying the world: rebuild a ghost only when its edit changes, not every tick, resolve the candidate without copying guests, and build the ghost mesh from only what the edit changes. On the full park, a path preview costs about 5 ms to make the candidate and 11 ms to build its ghost mesh per rebuild. Gated on: revising decision 0025, which makes previews exact through a whole-world copy, for the candidate part only.
- Texturing the paved surface, before any of it is built: bands that follow the outline inward, such as a sidewalk along each side of a wide path, and shapes or patterns laid on paths and plazas. Bands can come from the edge lines, which already run only along the outline of joined paving, but need a true inward offset, since today's strips only bevel at sharp turns. Patterns need world-space texture coordinates and the surface's pieces as their mask. If paths also get materials of their own, decide where joined paths of different materials overlap: whether their edge lines stay hidden, and which draws on top. A junction's paving belongs to its point, so it also needs a rule for which material it takes, or a split of its pieces among the segments it joins, as a corner's pieces are split. Joined paving must first be cut into regions that don't overlap: where a short segment lies inside a wider junction, its pieces stack on the junction's, so its material would cut into the wider path's (parks/busted-path.park, points 38 and 39).
- Guest types drawn by park appeal, as a later slice in the manner of Anno's tiers: thrill seekers, passholders who return often and come for theming, dining, and atmosphere, families, and diners, each arriving in proportion to how well the park serves them, never at a count threshold. Each type brings luxury needs that lower satisfaction and spending when unmet, such as sit-down restaurants fed by a kitchen, a bar serving alcohol to adults in the evening, and merch. Each new need asks supply lines for a new good, and types conflict through shared fields such as noise, theme, and spectacle. Gated on: money and arrivals that depend on the park.
- Park events to prepare for and react to, in the manner of RimWorld's incidents: school field trips, convention days, holidays, heatwaves, thunderstorms, supplier strikes, road closures at a service entrance, product recalls, power outages, and inspector visits. Each event changes something the systems already read, such as an arrival block of one guest type, a temperature field, or one good's lead time, and comes booked, forecast, seasonal, or sudden, so the player can prepare through policies. Self-caused events, such as breakdowns from wear or food poisoning from stale stock, come from the park's own state, never from a hidden director. Events are drawn by keyed draws from the park's seed. Gated on: the clock, guest types, and a weather field.
